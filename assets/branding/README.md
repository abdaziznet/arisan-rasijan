# BANI RASIJAN logo system

The mark is an **BR** monogram for **Bani Rasijan**. Its bold Plus Jakarta Sans letterforms stay legible at launcher-icon sizes, while the gold **R** gives the family name a warm, celebratory signature.

## Source-of-truth alignment

- Brand: **BANI RASIJAN** — *Arisan Keluarga*
- Category: private family arisan companion
- Audience: a single extended family, including older members
- Primary: emerald `#0F766E`; supporting primary-dark: `#115E59`
- Accent: warm gold `#D99A2B` (winner / celebration)
- Background: `#F8FAFC`; surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans; fallback Inter / system sans serif

## Files

- `bani_rasijan_app_icon.svg` — primary Play Store / launcher artwork, 1:1.
- `bani_rasijan_symbol.svg` — simplified BR monogram for compact contexts.
- `bani_rasijan_lockup_horizontal.svg` — light-background horizontal lockup.
- `bani_rasijan_lockup_vertical.svg` — light-background vertical lockup.

## Usage

The icon background is the emerald field; the foreground is the BR monogram. Keep it centered with at least 18% safe margin when exporting Android adaptive-icon layers. For monochrome, render the complete monogram in one color (`#0F766E` on light, `#F8FAFC` on dark) and omit the emerald icon background where needed.

Do not add gradients, shadows, extra text, or decorative symbols to the primary launcher icon. Gold is reserved for the **R** and celebratory contexts.
